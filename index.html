About

Our Ministry, Our Family

We are a ministry committed to seeing people come to know Christ and grow in faith together. Rooted in Scripture and marked by generosity, we meet in multiple locations so that no one has to travel far to worship the King who reigns forever.

Our heart is simple: one gospel, one family, many places. Wherever you are from, you are someone’s brother, sister, or neighbor here — and you are loved before you ever walk through our doors.
